## Summary

Implemented Phase 5 of the AI Roadmap: Advanced Generation. This release transforms the AI from a simple dictionary flashcard generator into an adaptive, intelligent tutor.

---

## What changed

### 1. Expert Tutor Explanations (`nuance` field)
- Updated Zod validation schemas for Gemini, OpenRouter, and Ollama to mandate a new optional `nuance` field.
- Instructed the AI to populate this field with grammar breakdowns, cultural context, and deeper explanations of *why* a word is used.
- Designed a beautiful `bg-indigo-50` floating banner in `StudyCardViewer` to display the Teacher's Note.
- Merged the nuance securely into the base `notes` field during `ImportDeck` serialization to ensure persistence in the core app.

### 2. Dynamic Difficulty Scaling
- Updated the AI Prompt Builder (`prompt-builder.ts`) to algorithmically analyze the length of the `context.known` array.
- If a beginner masters >15 concepts, the backend now silently overrides the static prompt to smoothly transition the generated output toward intermediate phrases and grammar without requiring manual user intervention.

### 3. Curriculum Progression
- Built `POST /api/v1/generate/curriculum` powered by `gemma-2-9b-it:free` via OpenRouter (or Gemini fallback).
- Added a new `Wand2` ("Next Topic") button in the AI Session Viewer header.
- Clicking the button analyzes your current memory and overarching goal, instantly popping open a banner suggesting the exact next logical topic to study along with a motivational reasoning string.

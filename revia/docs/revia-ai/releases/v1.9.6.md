## Summary

Full audit of the `/ai` section against the regular app UI (Dashboard, Decks, Explore, Settings, Practice/Review). Fixed the reported rating-footer overlap + long-text clipping in the shared swipe viewer, and aligned every AI screen (form header, settings card, streaming state, session banner, save-deck modal) with existing app patterns.

---

## What changed

### Shared viewer (`study-card-viewer.tsx`) — fixes Practice/Review/AI together
- **Footers slimmed**: `pb-10` → `pb-6 + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` on both hint and rating footers. Frees ~32px of card height on small phones; previous padding cleared a floating nav that no longer exists (app uses `FloatingNav` pill + `pb-28` shell).
- **Entry animation overlap**: `study-card-in` was `opacity + translateY(12px)` — during the 300ms entry the card sat 12px lower, visually sliding under the rating footer. Now opacity-only.
- **Unrevealed long-text clipping**: unrevealed bodies used `justify-center` + `overflow-y-auto` — a classic flexbox trap where overflowing content clips at the top and can't scroll to start. Replaced with an `m-auto` inner wrapper (`m-auto` centers when short, top-aligns + scrolls when tall). Applied to both swipe and ratings-mode paths.

### AI session viewer (`ai-session-viewer.tsx`)
- **Subtitle**: provider names collapsed to `ai` (`1 / 10 · beginner · ai`). Practice shows `N practiced · endless session`, Review shows `N of M due` — neither names infra, and the AI label was misleading (server cascades Ollama → OpenRouter silently).
- **Banner**: `bg-card/50` (translucent) → solid `bg-card` + explicit `border-border`, matching `Card` (`rounded-xl border bg-card`).

### Generator form (`ai-generator-form.tsx`)
- **Level/Batch grid**: `grid gap-3 sm:grid-cols-2` → `grid gap-3`. The app column is `max-w-md` (~428px); the `sm:` breakpoint fires inside it and crushed 3-button segmented controls. Regular UI never uses `sm:` grids inside the column (v1.7.2 cleanup).

### Streaming state (`ai-mode-content.tsx`)
- Was `space-y-8` + `text-2xl` + spinner Card. Now `space-y-6` + `text-3xl` + `PageSkeleton`-style skeleton rows (`h-8 w-40` title, `h-24` cards), matching Decks (`space-y-8`/`text-3xl`… note: Decks uses `space-y-8`, Settings/Explore use `space-y-6`/`space-y-8` mixed — AI now sits in that range) and the shared `PageSkeleton`.

### Save-deck modal (`save-deck-modal.tsx`)
- Already consistent (Card + `role=dialog` + z-[100] + bottom-sheet on mobile). No change needed.

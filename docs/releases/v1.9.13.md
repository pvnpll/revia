## Summary

Optimized the AI generation pipeline to fetch cards in a continuous stream. Instead of waiting for the queue to nearly deplete and then freezing the UI to fetch a large batch of 5-10 cards, the engine now fetches 10 cards initially, and then continuously fetches **1 card at a time in the background** whenever there are 6 or fewer cards remaining. This creates a seamless "infinite scroll" experience where generation perfectly syncs with the user's swiping speed.

---

## What changed

### Continuous Prefetch (`ai-mode-content.tsx`)
- Modified `handleInitialGenerate` to strictly default to 10 cards.
- Modified `handleNextBatch` to strictly fetch `batchSize = 1`.
- Because `batchSize` is 1, the `cardsRemaining <= 6` condition evaluates to true after every single swipe once the user reaches index 3, creating a perfectly balanced 1-for-1 continuous background pipeline.

### UI Simplification (`ai-generator-form.tsx`)
- Removed the `batchSize` selector from the AI Generator form since the pipeline is now fully automated and continuous.

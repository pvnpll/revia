## Summary

Rimev now has **two separate learning modes**: **Practice** (primary, endless adaptive sessions) and **Daily Review** (secondary, date-based spaced repetition). Opening the app lands you directly on a practice card. Deck and lesson flows use the same shared card viewer with independent scheduling logic.

---

## Added

- **Practice mode** — Endless adaptive review; cards re-enter the queue by rating (1–5), not by due date
- **`PracticeScheduler`** — Pure client-side queue logic with randomized gaps per rating (`src/lib/scheduler/practice-scheduler.ts`)
- **`DailyReviewScheduler`** — Wrapper for existing SRS used only by Daily Review (`src/lib/scheduler/daily-review-scheduler.ts`)
- **`GET /api/practice/cards`** — Fetch practice cards by deck, lesson, or recent decks
- **`PracticeSession`** component — Shared fullscreen viewer for practice (used on `/practice`, deck detail, lessons)
- **Recent-deck practice pool** — Default practice uses cards from your 5 most recently opened decks (all cards per deck, any lesson)
- **Deck access tracking** — Opening a deck or starting practice updates recency for the default pool
- **Distinct rating colors** — Minimal border tints for ratings 1–5 (red, violet, yellow, sky, emerald)
- **Unit tests** — `practice-scheduler.test.ts`

## Changed

- **App entry** — Login and `/` redirect to `/practice` with a card loaded immediately
- **Navigation order** — Dashboard → Practice → Decks → Explore
- **Daily Review** — Remains at `/review`; secondary CTA on dashboard (no longer the primary tab)
- **Lessons** — Tap a lesson opens Practice (lesson-scoped only); no longer updates SRS scheduling during lesson study
- **Deck practice** — “Practice deck” includes all non-suspended cards in the deck, regardless of lesson
- **Imported decks** — Visibility toggle hidden; only original authors control public sharing (API enforced)
- **Explore** — Public decks visible to everyone, including the author who published them
- **Settings copy** — Explore descriptions updated for inclusive public deck listing

## Fixed

- **Practice session init loop** — Resolved “Maximum update depth exceeded” when loading practice cards

---

## Architecture notes

| Mode | Scheduler | Persists SRS? | Queue behavior |
|------|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| **Practice** | `PracticeScheduler` | No | Endless; rating → reinsert after N cards |
| **Daily Review** | `DailyReviewScheduler` | Yes | Due cards only; finishes when caught up |

Both modes share `StudyCardViewer` and the same 1–5 rating UI.
